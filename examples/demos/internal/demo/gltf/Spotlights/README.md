# Spotlight stage

Original nt3d sample, generated by `../generate/main.go`. No third-party assets.
Run `go generate ./internal/demo/gltf` from `examples/demos` to regenerate.

- Three `KHR_lights_punctual` spotlights (warm gold, cyan, and magenta) ride an
  overhead carousel rig while pitching their gimbals to sweep cones across the stage,
  paired with an overhead `point` fill light.
- `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` lamp canisters sit on each spotlight node,
  glowing in sync with their beam directions (`-Z`).
- A counter-rotating brushed-gold PBR ring and three satin ceramic pillars cast
  moving perspective spotlight shadows across the stage floor.
- Authored perspective `"Stage front"` camera (`k` toggles between orbit and stage cameras).
