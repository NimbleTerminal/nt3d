Forest

TerrainMipmapsShapesSource ↗

Thousands of trees grow from three meshes, a pine, a round tree, and a bush, each made once with NewSharedGeometry and shown by every tree of its kind. The GPU uploads each once and draws all of a kind in view in one instanced call; the wind rewrites every tree's transform, and nothing else, each frame. Switch to separate copies to watch the frame time climb.
