3D Tiles

GaussiansLarge sceneSource ↗

An OGC 3D Tiles 1.1 tileset streaming into the scene: a generated town of 64 lots in three levels of detail, with a park of Gaussian splats. Tiles are chosen by how large their error looks on screen, so the lots in front show bases, towers and roofs while those behind stay plain blocks. Orbit and zoom to watch tiles load and give way.

The tileset is built in memory and read through nt3d/tiles3d; nt3d/tiles3d/scene fetches, decodes and places tiles in the background, each to the millimeter in an east-north-up frame on the Earth. The status line counts tiles shown, loaded and loading. GPU falls back to software when unavailable.
