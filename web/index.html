A GPU shader paints the surface before the scene renders. Watch the plasma animate, pause its phase, and compare the software fallback.

Dynamic scene ↗

Moving objects surround a deforming wave. Hold the mesh, add orbiters, or remove them while the scene keeps moving.

Shape builders ↗

Spheres, cylinders, cones, tori, and cuboids, lit and UV-mapped. Switch their normals between as built, flat, and smooth.

Dense mesh ↗

735,000 triangles: drawn whole in software, outlined in wireframe, and picked through an index built in the background.

07 / CUTOUT TEXTURES

Fur ↗

Spot the cow in shell fur colored by its own texture, swaying in the wind by moving nodes alone. Cycle the shell count to see the layers build strands.

08 / MIPMAPS

Mipmaps ↗

A tiled floor runs to the horizon. Turn mipmaps off to see far texels shimmer into moiré; on, the floor settles into an even tone, and anisotropic filtering keeps its detail toward the horizon, on the GPU and in software alike.

Explore the mipmaps →Source
09 / INSTANCING

Forest ↗

Thousands of trees from three shared meshes, each uploaded once and drawn in one instanced call, swaying in the wind. Switch to separate copies to compare.

Explore the forest →Source
10 / LIGHTING

Lights ↗

Shapes on a floor under presets of hemisphere ambient, a turning sun, and colored point lights orbiting, up to eight at once, lit per pixel on the GPU and in software alike.

Explore the lights →Source
11 / PARTICLES

Particles ↗

A fire, its smoke, and drifting dust of up to 100,000 points moved every frame: soft and sized in world units, glowing additively or blended by alpha in depth order, on the GPU and in software alike.

Explore the particles →Source

One scene, three renderers.

Press 1 for GPU, 2 for software, or 3 for wireframe. GPU rendering falls back to software when unavailable.

Prefer a native terminal?

From the repository, run task terrain, task procedural, task dynamic, task shapes, task models, task large, task fur, task mipmaps, task forest, task lights, task particles, task animation, or task animate in Kitty, or task meshes -- model.stl for your own files. Use --help for options.
