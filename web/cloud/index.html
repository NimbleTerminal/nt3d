Point cloud

ParticlesForestDense meshSource ↗

A galaxy of up to a million points, grouped once into clusters of 256. Each frame selects the clusters in view and a level of detail for each: every point nearby, a spread half or quarter farther out, and one point for a whole cluster far away. The status line counts the clusters and points drawn.
